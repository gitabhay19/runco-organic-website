30 video hook templates for founders

The first three seconds decide whether your video gets watched. Type in who you help and what they want. Every hook rewrites itself for your business.

Authority hooks

Lead with what you know. These work when your experience is the reason to listen.

  1. After years in B2B sales, here's what I'd tell every one of the founders I meet on day one.

  2. Three things I check first when founders ask me about more inbound leads.

  3. The question founders should ask about B2B sales, and almost never do.

  4. If I had to get more inbound leads from scratch, this is exactly where I'd start.

  5. What most founders get wrong about B2B sales, from someone who does it every day.

  6. Here's how I'd explain B2B sales to founders in under a minute.

Contrarian hooks

Disagree with the standard advice, then earn it in the next ten seconds.

  1. Stop trying to get more inbound leads the way everyone tells you to.

  2. Most advice about B2B sales makes things worse for founders. Here's why.

  3. You don't need more effort to get more inbound leads. You need to drop one habit.

  4. Everyone in B2B sales says do more. I tell founders to do less.

  5. The B2B sales advice I followed for years and now tell founders to ignore.

  6. Chasing more inbound leads isn't your real problem. This is.

Story hooks

Open in the middle of something that happened. Only tell stories that are true.

  1. A client came to me wanting more inbound leads. The first thing I did surprised them.

  2. The worst mistake I ever made in B2B sales, and what it taught me.

  3. Last week one of the founders I work with asked me a question I can't stop thinking about.

  4. I almost walked away from B2B sales. One conversation changed how I work.

  5. The day I realised most founders were being sold the wrong thing.

  6. Here's what happened when I stopped following the standard B2B sales playbook.

Proof hooks

Show a result, then explain it. Use these only with real numbers and real clients.

  1. Here's what changed for one of the founders I work with in 30 days.

  2. Before and after: what more inbound leads actually looks like.

  3. I'm going to show you the exact steps behind this result.

  4. This is the message a client sent me after we fixed their B2B sales.

  5. One change moved this client closer to more inbound leads. Here it is.

  6. People ask whether B2B sales really works for founders. Here's a real example.

How-to hooks

Promise one useful thing and deliver it fast.

  1. How to get more inbound leads without adding hours to your week.

  2. Three steps to more inbound leads that founders can start today.

  3. If you're one of the founders struggling with B2B sales, try this first.

  4. The five-minute B2B sales check I'd run before spending any more money.

  5. Do this before you hire anyone to help with B2B sales.

  6. Save this: a simple checklist for more inbound leads.

Turn a hook into a full video

A short video has three jobs, in this order.

  1. HookThe first three seconds. One line that names the viewer's problem or promises a result.
  2. HoldThe middle. One idea and one real example. Cut anything a customer wouldn't repeat.
  3. PayoffThe last line. Deliver what the hook promised, then give one next step.

Keep it true. Proof hooks only work with real clients and real numbers.
